How to play · Your first minute

1 · Explore & attackMove with arrow keys. Face a foe and tap F (fire), D (water), or S (grass). Select a spell card and click the field to aim. Rocks block movement and shots.

2 · Befriend all threeGet close and tap A toward a foe: each attempt has a 75% chance. No need to hurt it first. You have three slots; level one keeps one friend per element. Your discoveries stay checked even if a friend falls.

3 · Charge your magicEach violet energy orb powers one charged move. You can store up to five energy orbs. Hold F, D, or S for half a second for a fire pulse, water wave, or earthquake. Hold A to command your allies’ special moves. Each charged action uses one orb.

4 · Read the warningDodge a fire bird’s dash or stop it with water. Only grass hits a buried bush; move away from its eruption warning. Water slimes vanish and rain down on your last position: step away from the ripples. Tame these foes once they return to their normal state. Survive 60 seconds and tame all three elements to unlock level two.

Field drops · Collect within 10 seconds

Collect before they vanishCoins, bonus items, energy, health potions, and slot charms disappear after 10 seconds of play if uncollected. Pausing freezes this timer. Every defeated foe drops coins and has a 10% bonus-item chance.

◇ Element shield · 20sImmunity to the element of the foe that dropped it. While active, a circular elemental field surrounds the mage in that element's colour.

5 Rare union · 15sFive temporary helpers of the defeated foe’s element. They use no taming slots and do not count toward your lesson.

↗ Attack upgrade · Until replaced or defeatedRandomly gives three spreading shots or an exploding shot. Applies to regular elemental attacks and lasts until you collect another attack upgrade or die.

★ Ace · 8sYour spells hit every element, including buried or dashing foes, with bonus damage. You take no damage.

Violet energyEach orb can power one charged attack. You can keep up to five orbs in your inventory; extra orbs stay on the ground until their timer expires.

Coins & healthEvery defeated foe drops coins. Red health bottles appear around the clearing at random intervals and heal 35 health.
