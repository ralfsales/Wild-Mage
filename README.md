# 🧙‍♂️ Wild Mage

A pixel-art fantasy adventure where you play as a wandering mage exploring a wild magical world filled with elemental creatures, hidden treasures, and powerful spells.

Fight monsters, master elemental magic, tame enemies, and build your own magical army as you search for treasure and become the most powerful mage in the realm.

## ✨ Features

* 🔥 Fire Magic – Burn enemies over time.
* 💧 Water Magic – Launch water projectiles to damage foes.
* 🌿 Nature Magic – Use grass-based attacks against elemental opponents.
* 🐾 Creature Taming – Attempt to tame weakened monsters and turn them into allies.
* 💰 Coin Collection – Earn coins by defeating monsters and discovering hidden treasures.
* 🗺️ Open Exploration – Roam the map freely and discover secrets.
* 👾 Dynamic Monster Spawns – Creatures emerge from magical bushes throughout the world.
* 🎨 Retro Pixel-Art Style – Inspired by classic RPG and adventure games.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Controls

| Key               | Action        |
| ----------------- | ------------- |
| WASD / Arrow Keys | Move          |
| 1                 | Fire Spell    |
| 2                 | Water Spell   |
| 3                 | Nature Spell  |
| 4                 | Tame Creature |
| Space             | Interact      |

### Combat

Each monster belongs to an elemental type:

* 🔥 Fire
* 💧 Water
* 🌿 Grass

Use elemental advantages wisely to defeat enemies more efficiently.

### Taming System

When a monster falls below **50% HP**, you can attempt to tame it.

* Success Chance: **50%**
* On success, the creature becomes your ally.
* Allies automatically attack nearby enemies.
* Build a powerful team of tamed creatures to help on your adventure.

## 🏆 Objective

Explore the world and collect as many coins as possible by:

* Defeating monsters
* Finding hidden treasure chests
* Taming creatures to strengthen your party
* Surviving increasingly dangerous encounters
